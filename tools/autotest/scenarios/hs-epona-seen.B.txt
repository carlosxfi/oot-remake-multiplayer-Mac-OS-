# hs-epona-seen (Hookshot), B: the rider, on a save that has WON Epona (adult, Get_Epona Complete, Epona's Song
# known: offline/epona-seen-run.ps1 stages it as both games' save). A won save has no Epona standing in the field
# (she is placed only while Get_Epona is not Complete), so B calls her with Epona's Song, gets on, rides at walk,
# trot and gallop, stops, gets off, walks away and back, and gets on again. A stands 10 m south and watches
# (hs-epona-seen.A.txt: its marks shoot both games at the same moment, timed by A's own ride lines).
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Scenario hs-epona-seen (through offline/epona-seen-run.ps1).
# Pass lines (hookshot_log-B.txt / -A.txt), as the big run's epona-sync step:
#   b-owns       B min=1 coop: ride: Epona is ours \(Get_Epona Complete\)
#   b-free       B min=1 coop: ride: our Epona is out with nobody on her        (the song brought her)
#   b-rides      B min=1 coop: ride: we ride Animal_Epona_Adult
#   b-on-again   B min=1 coop: ride: our Epona has us on her again
#   a-rides      A min=1 coop: ride: P\d+ rides Animal_Epona_Adult
#   a-waits      A min=1 coop: ride: P\d+'s Animal_Epona_Adult waits, nobody on her \(the same copy\)
# VOID, not FAIL: b-free 0 (the song did not bring her: ocarina not on D-pad Left, or the notes missed), or
# b-rides 0 (no mount round got on: the shots after "epona-came" show where she stopped).
# Note keys, as Ocarina of Time 3D: A = D, R = F, Y = A, X = B, L = high D (N64: A, C-down, C-right, C-left, C-up).
# Epona's Song = high D, B, A twice = L X Y L X Y. Not the module's NoteOf (coop_ocarina_sound.cpp: L = D, A = high D,
# "inferred from the prompt; not heard"): runs epona-seen-0dacc172 (X Y R) and -r2 (A X Y, NoteOf's keys) were no song.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1,LICHII_COOP_OBJECTS=1,LICHII_COOP_CHEATS=1
#! timeout 900
mark start
wait 60
warp HyruleField_Adult HyruleField_FrontOfMarket
wait 1200               # the warp lands
mark field
place -99.0 151.0 264.5 # Lon Lon Ranch's corral, in the field's map (where Epona stands in a save that has not won her)
wait 240
face -99.0 290.0        # face north, away from A
press ZL                # camera behind B
wait 60
mark on-foot
press DLEFT 12          # the ocarina out
wait 240
mark ocarina
press L 12              # Epona's Song: high D, B, A, twice (L X Y L X Y)
wait 40
press X 12
wait 40
press Y 12
wait 40
press L 12
wait 40
press X 12
wait 40
press Y 12
wait 300
mark song
await 900 coop: ride: our Epona is out with nobody on her
press B 12              # the ocarina away if the song missed (else a sword swing on foot: nothing)
wait 480                # she runs up and stops near B
mark epona-came
# Mount rounds: she stops somewhere near B. Round 0 is where B stands; then a ring of 2 m, then of 3.5 m round

# (walk-*, trot-*, gallop-*) shoot both games while B holds the stick; no mark here lets the stick go.
stick 0.15 0.25 600     # walk (10 s)
