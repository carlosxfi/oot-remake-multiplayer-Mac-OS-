# hs-host-difficulty (Hookshot only; difficulty-lobby, C #114 plan B): the title's Host page before hosting has the
# Difficulty row (the lobby's, picked as it is made) and Custom's three sliders fit on the page. Game A starts co-op
# idle (`start A idle`: no server, as a player who has not hosted or joined), Host on the title (hs-title-menu.down /
# .press), then Difficulty Automatic -> Hard -> Custom with the pad (the change is kept for the next lobby).
# Shots (s<step>-<name>-A.png), judged by eye (no refs yet):
#   page      the Host page: Who can join, Players, Difficulty Automatic, Start hosting
#   hard      the Difficulty row says Hard
#   custom    the Difficulty row says Custom, its three sliders below it, Start hosting still on screen
#   closed    the title again
# Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Scenario hs-host-difficulty
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1
#! timeout 300
start A idle
sleep 30
lines rows-A A min=1 coop: title: the title's own rows Host, Join, Co-op Settings \(buttons 4 to 6\)$
lines idle-A A max=0 coop: welcomed as player
play A hs-title-menu.down.txt
play A hs-title-menu.press.txt
shot page
play A hs-host-difficulty.hard.txt
shot hard
lines kept-A A min=3 coop: multiplayer tab: difficulty kept for the next lobby
play A hs-host-difficulty.custom.txt
shot custom
lines kept-custom-A A min=4 coop: multiplayer tab: difficulty kept for the next lobby
lines refused-A A max=0 coop: multiplayer tab: difficulty refused
play A hs-title-menu.close.txt
shot closed
