# hs-ocarina (Hookshot, issue 152): ocarina-notes on Hookshot. Before hs-ocarina the module had
# no pad to read, so A sent no notes. Now it reads the word Hookshot's nn::hid gave the game
# (our patch's HosCoopApi::pad_buttons). Run: tools\autotest\run.ps1 -Runtime hookshot -Scenario hs-ocarina
# Needs Hookshot built again with this branch's patch (the module's start line has no
# "gives no pad word" note). Proof, A's log: "coop: ocarina note 4 (Ocarina_D) sent" ...
# "note 16 (Ocarina_HighD) sent", "note 0 (let go) sent" after each; B's log as in ocarina-notes
# ("P1 plays sound 'Ocarina_D'"). B only waits.
# Big-run rows 15-16 (ocarina-notes, 2026-11-14): A walks ahead, then turns to face B's camera,
# so B's shots at note-L..note-A show the puppet's front. B's log: "P1: Ocarina notes go to
# sound user 0x... 'Ocarina' (... field +0x40 -> +0x8)" once, then "P1 plays sound
# 'Ocarina_D' (returned 0x..)" per note, and no "is not in its sound user"; "P1 ocarina to
# the hand ('DemoOcarina')". B's shots at note-*: the Ocarina in the puppet's right hand at
# its face, no shield or sword in its hands; after-song/end: Ocarina back on the belt.
#! switches LICHII_COOP_PUPPET=1,LICHII_COOP_DEBUG_ANIM=1
mark start
wait 120
hold LSTICK_UP 40
wait 60
# Turn round to face B (a short tap turns Link where he stands).
hold LSTICK_DOWN 4
wait 60
mark facing
press DLEFT 12
wait 240
mark out
# The first Ocarina use shows an "Ocarina Audio Input" note that eats L R Y X; A closes it
# (the A press may also send one HighD note before the test notes).
press A 12
wait 120
mark ready
mark note-L
hold L 30
wait 90
mark note-R
hold R 30
wait 90
mark note-Y
hold Y 30
wait 90
mark note-X
hold X 30
wait 90
mark note-A
hold A 30
wait 90
mark lullaby
song ZeldaLullaby  # keys from tools/autotest/ocarina_notes.py (Y X A Y X A)
wait 300
mark after-song
press B 12
wait 120
press B 12
wait 90
mark end
